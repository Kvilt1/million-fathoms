    Component map

    1. 1
      Title → hull nameplate

      A black plate with four screws and edge-lit lettering, plus a stencilled hull number and station line underneath.

    2. 2
      Stats → gauge cluster

      One instrument housing holding four LED repeaters. Each reading has a red embossed label tape above its window. Best EP glows amber.

    3. 3
      Sound button → guarded toggle

      A lever switch under a striped guard, with a status LED. Muted means the lever is down and the LED is dark.

    4. 4
      Depth ruler → depth tape

      A black scale tape with fathom numbers, the sounding line from the surface, and a pointer box that reads out the lead's depth.

    5. 5
      Reels → fathometer

      Dot-matrix tubes behind smoked glass in a double bezel. The unlit 8 shows faintly behind every digit, and lit digits burn white-hot.

    6. 6
      EP total → return scope

      An oscilloscope screen with a graticule. The sonar return trace spikes on each badge as it lands, and the EP counter reads out in phosphor.

    7. 7
      Tier label → annunciator panel

      Replaces the stamp. All seven tiers sit as tinted lamps, and the roll's tier lights up. Each lamp lights in turn as the counter climbs past its floor.

    8. 8
      Roll → dive button

      A lit red push-button inside a hazard-striped guard ring. The lamp dims while the reels spin and flashes on the result.

    9. 9
      Speed → speed order dial

      An octagonal rotary selector, like a ship's engine-order telegraph. It clicks between Normal and Fast.

    10. 10
      Badge list → sonar contacts

      Each badge is logged as a contact with an ID and a signal-strength meter: one bar per tier step. The top contact gets a bracket.

    11. 11
      Menu → bulkhead switch panel

      Black station keys under small indicator LEDs. An LED lights when that section has something new, like a fresh badge or set.

    12. ·
      Not on this screen

      Drawers open as maintenance hatches with a stencilled frame. Toasts print out as teletype strips. The Mythic flash goes to a full red blackout, then a white flood.

    Tier ramp

    Everything else is red, so these are the only other colours on screen. Rare and Epic move off red so no tier matches the base.

    What changes when we build it

    • styles.css: black hardware tokens replace the navy ones, plus new --t0…--t6. The label tape, screws and bezels become shared classes.
    • App.tsx: small markup additions for the plate, the gauge windows, the toggle, the annunciator row and the dial. The stamp element becomes the annunciator.
    • Haul rows (parts.tsx): a contact ID and a signal meter are added to each row, derived from the badge's tier.
    • scene.ts: the grid becomes the sonar scope with a sweep, and badge hits ping as blips. The depth tape is drawn where the ruler is today.
    • Return scope: a small canvas trace in the readout, driven by the reveal that already happens.
    • Fonts: Doto is added for the LED readouts, inlined like VT323 (about 30 KB). VT323 stays for everything else. The style section of CLAUDE.md gets updated to match.
    • Motion: reduced motion freezes the sweep, the trace and the blips. The effects setting keeps covering only shake and particles.
